Classic 1995 mockup

Real size at the bottom, 2.5x copy above. Click Start for the menu. Hover the clock for the date. Icons are placeholders, the app pulls each app's own icon.

Classic 1995 is the grey, square look: raised buttons, a pressed button for the active window, a sunken tray and a Start menu with a side banner. The panels at the top right are the tray panels, the calendar and the group list in the same style. Switch the theme to compare with the other two.
