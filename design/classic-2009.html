Classic 2009 mockup

Real size at the bottom, 2.2x copy above. Click the round Start button for the menu, then the arrow next to Shut Down for Sleep and Log Out. Hover a window button for the glow, which takes its color from the app's icon. Icons are placeholders, the app pulls each app's own icon.

Classic 2009 is the dark glass look: a round Start button, big icons with no titles, pins sharing the row with open windows, and a Start menu with a white list on the left and links on the glass on the right. The glass is tinted by the accent color and ignores light and dark, like Classic 1995. The panels at the top right are the tray panels and the group list.

Questions

  2. Show Desktop strip at the right end. Click hides every window, click again brings them back. It's new, so it'd be a setting.
  3. Shut Down with an arrow for Sleep and Log Out, or three buttons like the other themes?
  4. Picking this theme switches the bar to icons only at 40 px once, or leaves those settings alone?
